02 — HOW I WORK

Approach

Vision

To turn data into decisions — building marketing that is measurable, creative, and grounded in what actually moves the needle for a brand, a campaign, or a business unit.

Mission

  • Combine performance marketing with data-driven optimization across paid media, SEO, and analytics.
  • Collaborate across teams — creative, product, and business units — to align messaging and priorities.
  • Build automated reporting and dashboards that make performance visible and actionable.
  • Bring proactive, detail-oriented execution to every campaign, brand, and stakeholder relationship.
